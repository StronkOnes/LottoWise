# LottoWise User Guide

Welcome to **LottoWise**, a professional-grade lottery analysis and prediction dashboard. This application combines cryptographic randomness with advanced statistical modeling to provide insights into lottery patterns and generate optimized playing systems.

## Core Engines

### 1. ISAAC Cryptographic Generator
Unlike standard random number generators that might only cover a fraction of the combinatoric space, LottoWise uses the **ISAAC** (Indirection, Shift, Accumulate, Add, and Count) algorithm. 
- **Entropy Source:** 1,024 bytes of system noise (`os.urandom`).
- **Full Coverage:** Ensures that every single one of the ~14 million possible combinations in a 6/49 game is reachable.

### 2. Markov Chain Modeling
The prediction engine analyzes the "transition states" between historical draws.
- **Probabilistic Transitions:** If number X appears in one draw, the engine calculates the probability of number Y appearing in the next, based on over 5,800 historical data points.
- **Weighted Selection:** Picks are generated by weighting the ISAAC randomness with these Markov probabilities.

### 3. Delta System Filter
The engine applies the **Delta System**, a gap-analysis theory:
- It analyzes the spacing between adjacent numbers in a draw.
- **Rules:** Enforces patterns like "the first delta is usually low (1-3)" and "most deltas are under 15."

## Dashboard Features

- **Real-Time Stats:** View "Sizzling Hot" and "Frozen Cold" numbers based on live historical data.
- **Frequency Analysis:** Interactive bar charts showing the distribution of drawn numbers.
- **Co-occurrence Heatmap:** A 2D visualization revealing which number pairs "like" to appear together.

## System Builder & The Exclusiveness Condition

The **System Builder** is the most advanced part of the app. It generates a group of tickets (a "system") designed to maximize your winning probability.

### What is the Exclusiveness Condition?
Standard lottery tickets often overlap (e.g., two tickets sharing 3 or 4 numbers). While this can lead to multiple small prizes, it **decreases your overall probability** of hitting at least one win.

LottoWise enforces the **Exclusiveness Condition**:
- **Mathematical Formula:** $c_{ij} \le 2k - n - 1$
- **$c_{ij}$:** Maximum common numbers allowed between any two tickets.
- **$k$:** Your target win threshold (e.g., Match 4).
- **$n$:** Draw size (e.g., 6).

By keeping the tickets "exclusive" (minimizing overlap), the engine ensures that your winning probability grows **linearly** with every ticket you play.
